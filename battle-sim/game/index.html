HOW TO READ THE DATA

OVR, attributes and S–G grades are fan-made gameplay ratings. Primary and secondary badges show project-compatible roles. Demonstrated canon roles are recorded separately in dossiers. Chemistry interprets recorded relationships; it is not an official Blue Lock statistic.
