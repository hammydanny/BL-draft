5. Privacy & Browser Storage

The simulator is designed as a local browser experience and does not require an account. To provide its features, it stores certain data in your browser's local storage, which can include:

  • Saved auction progress, team names, budgets, player selections, and auction history.
  • Formation assignments, captain selections, and standalone team-builder choices.
  • Display and sound preferences, such as the selected theme and sound setting.

This local data generally remains in the same browser profile on the same device until you clear it, remove the site's storage, or use browser settings that clear site data. Clearing it may remove saved games and preferences. The project application is not designed to submit your saved game state to a project-operated game server.

If you access a hosted version, the hosting provider and your browser or network may process technical connection information as part of delivering the website. That processing is governed by the relevant provider's own policies, which this project cannot control.
