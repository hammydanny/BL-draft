Player OVR, attributes, S–G grades and chemistry scores are fan-made gameplay values. Project-compatible positions and tactical interpretations are distinguished from demonstrated canon-role references in dossiers. Character profiles retain the project’s recorded reference information; a missing role or quote is valid. They are not official Blue Lock statistics, canon rankings or statements by the original creators.

05 / Privacy & browser data

The current app stores Auction progress, squad and formation choices, Captains, and preferences in your browser. It does not provide account-based cloud saves or submit saved game state to a project-operated game server.

Clearing browser site data can remove saves and preferences. Hosting infrastructure may process technical connection information when delivering the website. Read the Privacy Notice for storage, cache, external-link and data-control details.
