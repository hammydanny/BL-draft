BLUE LOCK DRAFT // PRIVACY INFORMATION

Privacy & Browser Data

Last updated: 9 October 2026

1. Overview

Blue Lock Draft is designed as a local, browser-based fan simulator. You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other personal details to use its core features. This notice explains what information may be stored in your browser and what may be processed when the website is hosted and delivered to you.

This is a plain-language description of the project's intended behaviour, not a guarantee about every browser, hosting platform, extension, or network you may use.

2. Information Stored in Your Browser

The simulator uses your browser's local storage to remember information between visits. Depending on the features you use, this can include:

  • Saved auction sessions, team names, budgets, player selections, and auction history.
  • Formation and captain assignments, plus standalone team-builder choices.
  • Site preferences such as the selected theme and sound setting.

This data is intended to remain in the same browser profile on the same device. It is not a cloud save and may not follow you to another browser, device, or private-browsing session. The application is not designed to submit saved game state to a project-operated game server.

Local storage is not an encrypted vault. Anyone with access to your browser profile or device may be able to access information stored there, so avoid putting passwords, contact details, or other sensitive information into team names or other saved fields.

3. Information Processed by Hosting Providers

When you visit a hosted website, your browser must request the page and its supporting files. The hosting platform or related infrastructure may process technical connection data such as your IP address, browser or device information, the requested URL, referral information, and the date and time of a request. Such processing may be recorded in service logs according to the provider's own policies.

The project maintainers do not control the hosting provider's infrastructure or retention practices. For information about those records, consult the privacy documentation of the platform serving the version of the site you visit.

4. Cookies, Analytics & Advertising

The simulator's own save and preference features are based on browser local storage. The project is not designed to require sign-in cookies, build advertising profiles, or sell personal information. Your browser, hosting platform, privacy extensions, or any third-party site you open may behave independently of this application.

If the project adds analytics, embedded services, accounts, or other features that materially change how data is handled, this notice should be revised to describe those changes.

5. Third-Party Links

The site may link to third-party services, including GitHub for source code and project discussion. When you follow an external link, that service may collect or process information under its own terms and privacy policy. The project cannot control another site's security, content, or data practices.

6. Your Choices & Deleting Local Data

You can remove saved simulator data by clearing this site's local storage or site data in your browser's settings. The exact steps vary by browser. Clearing it may permanently remove unfinished auctions, saved teams, formations, and preferences; export or otherwise preserve anything you need before clearing it if the current version offers that option.

You can also stop using the site at any time. No account needs to be closed. Browser privacy controls, network settings, or extensions may affect how the page loads and what technical information is handled by your browser or service provider.

7. Retention & Security

Browser-stored saves generally remain until you clear them, remove the site's stored data, or use a browser feature that clears it automatically. The project does not provide a separate account dashboard or server-side save system for viewing or deleting those local saves.

No website or local storage mechanism can be promised to be completely secure in every environment. Keep your browser and device protected and do not save sensitive personal information in the simulator.

8. Updates & Contact

This notice may be revised as features or hosting arrangements change. The revision date at the top indicates when this page was last updated.

For privacy questions or to raise a concern about project content, contact the maintainers through the project repository on GitHub. Please do not put private or sensitive information in a public issue.
