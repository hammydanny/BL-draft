01 / The short version

Blue Lock Draft is a local, browser-based fan project. No account, email address or other personal details are required to use its core features. Team names can be any labels you choose.

NO ACCOUNTSLOCAL SAVESNO ANALYTICS

This notice describes the current website. Your browser, extensions, network and hosting provider may process information separately.

02 / What the website stores

The app uses browser localStorage to remember your progress and choices:

  • Auction: team names and colors, budgets, player pool, rosters, Auction progress and history, Undo / Redo state, formations and Captain assignments.
  • Standalone Team Builder: selected players, formation, positions, Captain and saved display choices.
  • Preferences: Dark / Light / System theme and sound effects.

These values remain in your browser profile unless you deliberately share a generated report or otherwise export or send information yourself. The current app does not transmit saved game state to a project-operated game server or provide cloud synchronization.

A save belongs to the browser profile and site address where you created it. It may not appear on another device, browser or private-browsing session.

03 / Cookies, analytics & tracking

Blue Lock Draft's own application code does not set cookies. Saves and preferences use localStorage instead.

The current app does not include analytics, behavioral tracking, advertising trackers or an advertising-profile system. This release adds none. Hosting providers and external websites have their own practices; this notice does not promise that they never set cookies or process connection data.

04 / Hosting & cached files

Your browser requests pages, code, styles, fonts and images from the site host. Hosting infrastructure may process technical information such as your IP address, requested URL, browser details, referrer and request time, under the provider's own policies. The project does not control those logs or retention periods.

The website's service worker can cache images and fonts on your device to speed up later visits. It does not indefinitely store old HTML, JavaScript or CSS. Browser cache and site-storage controls can remove these local copies.

05 / External links & third-party content

Links to GitHub and other external sites leave Blue Lock Draft. Those services operate independently and apply their own terms and privacy policies. Review them before sharing personal information.

Blue Lock names, characters, imagery and related intellectual property belong to their respective rights holders. Their use here does not imply affiliation or endorsement. See the Legal Notice for project and rights information.

06 / Control your local data

Change preferences: use Settings to choose your theme and SFX. Reset Preferences restores Dark mode and SFX on; it leaves your Auction and standalone Builder saves intact.

Remove an Auction: use the saved-session discard action in Auction Setup. It asks for confirmation and removes that Auction save.

Remove all locally stored data: open your browser's site settings for the address you use and clear its site data or local storage. Browser wording varies. This can permanently remove unfinished Auctions, saved squads, formations, preferences and cached files. Keep any reports you need first; a shared report is not a restorable game-save backup.

No account needs to be closed. Browser privacy tools or storage restrictions can also prevent saves or change how files load.

07 / Retention & security

Local saves usually remain until you clear them, the browser removes site data, or an in-app action replaces or discards the relevant save. The project has no separate server-side save dashboard or account-retention system.

localStorage is not an encrypted vault. Anyone with access to your device or browser profile may be able to read it. Avoid using passwords, contact details or other sensitive information as team names. No security certification or universal security guarantee is claimed.
