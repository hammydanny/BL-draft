# v0.6.9.5 roster alias and quote audit

> Correction review, 2026-10-15: the current audit is [v0.6.9.5-final-correction.md](v0.6.9.5-final-correction.md). This earlier ledger omitted Sendo’s official ace title and is retained as historical provenance. Current total: 84 aliases; every previous valid record is retained.

Reviewed 2026-10-14. Scope: all 79 players in the current-main roster, through chapter 364 (including its second part released 2026-10-14). The publisher release listing was checked at https://pocket.shonenmagazine.com/title/00617/episode/445051 . Future chapters and fan speculation were excluded.

Preserved 61 existing sourced aliases and added 19 corroborated aliases/titles, for 80 total. Each alias retains its individual verification context in `js/player-reference.js`. All display translations use the shared catalog; player names and IDs remain unchanged. Builder cards and selected-player panels omit aliases; Database dossiers and searches retain them.

An empty row means no alias was independently established in the reviewed material. It is not a claim that the character could never have a nickname. General descriptions, weapons, and transient adjectives were not automatically promoted into aliases. Wiki entries were treated as leads requiring context, not as sufficient evidence for additions.

## Deliberate exclusions

God of Glam was not asserted as a second separate Aryu nickname: the publisher Q&A directly establishes 美の巨人, rendered here as Giant of Beauty. Uncorroborated Shark claims and other wiki-only candidate titles were not added. Kiyora’s Dance Battle Footballer was added only after inspecting the official publisher image. Sae’s Japanese epithet was corroborated through the broadcaster’s profile, its explicit EGOIST BIBLE attribution, and the broadcaster’s own title usage.

The older `PLAYER_REFERENCE_CONTENT_REVIEW` ledger is retained as historical provenance. This document records the v0.6.9.5 alias/quote delta separately.
