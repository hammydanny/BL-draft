# v0.8.9.3 — Battle Sim kits in player dossiers

- The player dossier's Football Weapons section now also lists a "Battle Sim kit" for every player with Battle Sim abilities: passives, signature moves, Flow states and chemical reactions, each with its plain "when / what / limit" description. Entries already shown on a football weapon card are not repeated. This makes the full kits of Kira, Noa, Bachira, Igarashi and Kunigami (and Isagi) visible; Kira, who has no canon weapons, shows only his Battle Sim kit.
- Fixed chemistry links shown in player dossiers.
- Kit labels (BATTLE SIM KIT, PASSIVE, SPECIAL, ULTIMATE, FLOW, CHEMICAL REACTION) are translated in all 11 languages.
- The changelog text has been reworded so that it only describes public content.
