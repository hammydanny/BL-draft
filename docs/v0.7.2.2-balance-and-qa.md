# v0.7.2.2 balance and verification

Based on latest main `8333962d6cde286f0725be4a6b33485153fd5e13` (read-only; no commit, push or PR).

## What changed

- Daily begins with three formation choices. Twelve purpose-built layouts cover 7, 8, 9 and 10 players; 11-player/14-player drafts retain full-size formations. Formation choice changes the required roles and seeded offers. Two Ego Tokens and pick-one-of-three play remain intact.
- Mini-formations use the existing position semantics and shared solver. Their explicit shape/edge registries leave all ten original formation definitions and chemistry edges unchanged. Exact small-sided geometry survives squad codes, local saves, Builder handoff and PNG export.
- One shared model separates immutable base OVR, positional contribution and bounded contextual chemistry. Auction planning, Daily, puzzles, Auto Best, Builder and results consume that model.
- CPU values actual upgrades, missing roles, useful versatility, public scarcity, chemistry and the remaining completion budget. No extra money, future-order access, fixed public player prices, or changes to bid increments.
- Builder shows a selected player's base/position/chemistry/effective breakdown, compact move deltas and a touch-visible result after moving. Existing yellow/green fit and blue swap hints remain intact.
- Results distinguish base quality and effective quality. Their verdict uses effective contribution and squad completeness rather than one vote per stat. PNG player labels show base → effective; the preview remains compact, with reserves only below the pitch.
- Related repairs: the new split-player loader is exercised by preflight; the shared header consistently retains the disabled Battle Sim link; missing optional badge rendering no longer crashes Auction; tiny inherited labels are readable; occupied mini-pitches fit 320px result cards; new CJK glyphs are included while retaining all original glyphs and variable font weights.

## Balance

The internal value curve is `exp((OVR - 70) / 18)`, converted back to familiar OVR units for squad displays. The weakest third retains a quarter of lineup-quality weight. No player rating is edited and no extra power score appears in the interface.

Primary, explicit secondary and compatible roles are distinguished without changing eligibility. Attribute-based role adjustments are limited to ±3. Secondary/compatible roles have small costs; unsuitable same-group/cross-group placements lose 7/14, with larger 30/35 costs for inappropriate goalkeeper use. The existing demonstrated-position data remains authoritative.

Chemistry uses actual active tactical neighbours: 55% local mean, 30% strongest two links and 15% weakest link, followed by a bounded curve. Representative effects are 55 → −2, 65 → −1, 72 → 0, 82 → +1, 90 → +2.2, 95 → +3.2 and 100 → +4. Additional links never stack unrestricted bonuses. Base player data and relationship scores are unchanged. Database positional previews use neutral chemistry because no squad context exists.

Tests verify that 88 with excellent chemistry can beat 91 with poor chemistry, while 95 with neutral chemistry remains ahead of 80 with perfect chemistry. Auto Best evaluates every full formation and uses the same bounded contributions; small-sided handoffs choose within their requested format.

In a controlled 45-auction CPU-versus-CPU comparison (three difficulties, three public pools, five seeds), paid elite signings averaged 1,225 versus 1,015 for 80–89 OVR players, compared with 1,143 versus 1,074 in the unchanged baseline. The elite premium rose from about 6% to 21%, while the overall paid mean stayed nearly unchanged (953 → 956). Free awards were excluded. These are sample outcomes, not guaranteed prices.

## Compatibility

All per-character files, flags, aliases, quotes, reference metadata and the original formation/relationship definitions were compared with main and preserved. Shigeo Mizuki's quote and its translations remain intact. Existing auction/standalone save keys and schemas remain compatible. Older Daily attempts retain drafted players, remaining rerolls, their original squad size and a still-deployed captain; subsequent actions use the new deterministic format. Historical best records are retained rather than erased; scores across balance versions are not directly equivalent.

The existing Battle Sim source and abilities are preserved and remain disabled. This patch adds no Match Battle or Gauntlet gameplay.
